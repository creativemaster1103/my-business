# The four gates

**Nothing gets written until all four are resolved.** Persona, Awareness Level, Core Desire, and
Ad Angle. They are a gate, not a worksheet: a script started before they are settled is a script
written to nobody in particular, and it reads that way.

> ## All four are the user's to set — if one is missing, ASK
>
> **Do not choose a gate the user did not give you.** All four, Ad Angle included. Naming a
> persona answers one gate and leaves three open.
>
> **Recommending is not deciding.** Everything in this file is for arriving at the question well
> prepared: two to four options, drawn from the persona's own pain / desire / fear, with a
> recommendation and the reason. Then **wait for the answer**. Picking one and reporting it
> afterwards hands the user a review instead of a decision, and by then the script is already
> built on it.
>
> Ask for all the missing gates in **one exchange**, not four.

State all four explicitly, per script, before the first line. Both scripts in a run get their own
set — and they must not land on the same Persona + Angle pairing.

## Gate 2 — Awareness level

Where the viewer already is when the ad interrupts them. It decides **what the first line is
allowed to assume**, and getting it wrong is the most common reason a good script underperforms.

| Level | They know | The script opens on | It fails when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unaware** | Nothing. Not even that this is a problem | A situation they recognise, named precisely | You mention the product |
| **Problem-Aware** | They feel it, have not looked for a fix | The pain, in their words | You lead with mechanism |
| **Solution-Aware** | A category like this exists | Why *this* one, versus the thing they tried | You re-explain the problem they already know |
| **Product-Aware** | They know VeRelief | The objection holding them back | You sell benefits they have already read |
| **Most-Aware** | Ready, waiting on a reason | The offer and the nudge | You warm them up all over again |

Read it off the persona rather than guessing. The Official Persona page states it in the buying
behaviour, often verbatim: the **HRV Hunter** is *"solution-aware, so they price- and
mechanism-compare hard"*; the **Off-Ramper** *"researches mechanism before buying"*; the
**Lights-Out Loser** is the *"lowest-friction entry point… the largest, least-stigmatized desire
in the base, which is its strategic strength for cold traffic"* — that is a Problem-Aware, cold
audience read.

**Cold UGC is usually Unaware or Problem-Aware.** If you have written a script that assumes the
viewer knows what vagal stimulation is, you have written a retargeting ad. That is fine, as long
as it was the decision and not an accident.

## Gate 3 — Core Desire

The persona's **TOP DESIRE** is the specific want. The Core Desire is the **drive underneath it**
— and the same Notion page carries the reference: the **`📚 Life Force 8`** toggle.

1. Survival, enjoyment of life, life extension
2. Enjoyment of food and beverages
3. Freedom from fear, pain, and danger
4. Sexual companionship
5. Comfortable living conditions
6. To be superior, winning, keeping up with the Joneses
7. Care and protection of loved ones
8. Social approval

Name **one**. Two means it is not resolved.

Typical mappings — confirm against the live page rather than inheriting these:

| Persona | Usual core desire | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Self-Rescuer | **3** — freedom from fear, pain, danger | The most direct LF8 fit in the set |
| Wired Lifer | **1** or **6** | *"chronic stress is physically destroying them"* is 1, not calm. The ROI framing is 6 |
| Lights-Out Loser | **5** — comfortable living conditions | Restful sleep is named in LF5 explicitly |
| Chronic Condition Warrior | **1** — survival, enjoyment of life | "Spoons back" is life, not comfort |
| HRV Hunter | **6** — to be superior, winning | Pride in being right, not relief. Emotionally cooler |
| Off-Ramper | **3** — freedom from fear | Fear of dependence is the master emotion |
| Clinician | **8** — social approval | Professional credibility is the currency |
| Gift / Caregiver | **7** — care and protection of loved ones | Straight LF7 |

**Why it is a gate:** the Core Desire is what the CTA has to pay off. A script that opens on
LF6 pride and closes on LF3 relief loses the viewer at the turn, and no amount of hook
testing fixes it.

## Gate 4 — Ad Angle

**Ask, like the other three.** What is different about this gate is not that you may decide it —
it is that the persona page gives you enough to arrive with good candidates rather than an open
question. Derive two to four, recommend one, say why, and let the user pick.

Derive them like this:

- **From the PAIN** → Problem-Solution, Before & After, the cost of doing nothing
- **From the DESIRE** → Transformation, Identity, Functional Benefit
- **From the FEAR** → Objection-first, Comparison, Authority, Risk-reversal
- **From FAILED SOLUTION** → Us vs Them, "why I switched", "stop wasting your X"

Worked examples, straight off the persona page:

| Persona | Pulled from | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Wired Lifer | Fear: *"another wellness purchase that ends up in a drawer"* | Objection-first — open on the drawer |
| Lights-Out Loser | Fear: *"doubt that five minutes of anything could beat a pill"* | Comparison — five minutes vs the pill, without naming a drug |
| HRV Hunter | Fear: *"no objective readout means it's indistinguishable from placebo"* | Authority / demonstration — the dashboard is the proof |
| Off-Ramper | Desire: *"handle an emergency without reaching for the benzo"* | **Do not run this as-is.** Reframe to control and body state — the medication framing is the flagged risk |
| Self-Rescuer | Failed solution: *breathwork, box breathing* | Us vs Them — the tool that does not need composure you do not have |

**Check the chosen angle against the exclusions** in `config/competitors.yml` → `excluded_angles`
before writing. If the user picks one that collides with an exclusion, say so and offer the
nearest workable alternative rather than silently reshaping it.


Prescription/medication replacement, named-condition treatment, ingestible dosing and
subscription-only mechanics do not transfer to this product, whoever the persona is.

## Recording the gates

Once the user has set them, restate them back in the **chat report**, one block per script, and
mark which came from the user and which you recommended and they approved:

```
Script 1
  Persona          The Lights-Out Loser (Sleep Seeker) — approved set
  Awareness        Problem-Aware (cold)
  Core Desire      LF5 — comfortable living conditions
  Ad Angle         Comparison (suggested — from TOP FEAR: "five minutes vs a pill")
```

**They do not go in the brief.** The creator cannot act on an awareness level, and the UGC Brief
Database has no properties to file them in. They are the reasoning that produced the script, and
they belong where Mark can check the reasoning — in chat, next to the customer quote the script
was built on.
